## Substack

- **Notes feed no longer prioritizes followed accounts** (changed late
  2025) — most of what a user sees now comes from creators they've never
  followed, matched via audience-overlap signals (shared subscriptions,
  reading history). Corroborated across multiple independent 2026 sources:
  [niusleters.com](https://niusleters.com/en/blog/substack-2026-whats-changed-whats-working-and-whats-not),
  [reallygoodbusinessideas.com](https://www.reallygoodbusinessideas.com/p/substack-notes-algorithm).
- **Restacks/replies as the dominant growth signal**: Substack's Head of ML,
  Mike Cohen, stated the feed is optimized for subscription conversion, not
  dwell time, and that on-platform behaviors (restacking, replying,
  quote-sharing) let Substack "understand the full life cycle of behavior
  and help intersect audiences" —
  [pubstacksuccess.substack.com](https://pubstacksuccess.substack.com/p/the-notes-algorithm-explained-by).
  A single-account self-reported analysis (9,641 Notes, not independently
  verified, directional only) found restacks converted to subscribers ~12x
  more effectively than likes —
  [writebuildscale.substack.com](https://writebuildscale.substack.com/p/i-analyzed-9641-substack-notes-heres).
- **Links don't suppress Substack reach** — repeated across sources as
  platform philosophy (Substack's business model depends on driving reads),
  though no rigorous A/B test found:
  [kucharski.substack.com](https://kucharski.substack.com/p/how-much-does-x-suppress-substack).
- **No official length preference** — Cohen states format doesn't matter to
  the algorithm; long essays, one-liners, and video "compete equally."
  Practitioner claims of an ideal length are folklore, not platform-confirmed.
- **Visual Notes trending up**: ~1 in 3 Notes now include photo/video, share
  rising; Substack redesigned its media tab as an Instagram-style feed in
  2026 —
  [niusleters.com](https://niusleters.com/en/blog/substack-2026-whats-changed-whats-working-and-whats-not).
- **Recommendations**: Substack-reported figures — 78% of new subscribers to
  recommending publications' referred newsletters come from other Substack
  recommendations; publications using Recommendations grow ~2.75x faster on
  average —
  [writebuildscale.substack.com](https://writebuildscale.substack.com/p/how-id-grow-on-substack-fast-in-2026).
- **"Dinner party" framing**: Substack's own team frames passive publishing
  (posting without replying/restacking others) as the top failure mode —
  same source as the ML-lead quote above.
- **Substack SEO fields**: SEO Title, SEO Description, post slug, social
  preview image are all editable; no canonical URL control, no sitemap
  control beyond manual GSC re-indexing —
  [on.substack.com/p/substack-seo-guide](https://on.substack.com/p/substack-seo-guide),
  [dellos.in](https://dellos.in/seo-limitations-of-substack-and-how-to-fix-them/).

## LinkedIn

- **External-link penalty**: ~60% reach reduction vs. link-free posts,
  repeated across independent 2026 sources —
  [dataslayer.ai](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now).
- **Link-in-first-comment workaround is dead as of early 2026** — the
  algorithm now detects the "bridge behavior" pattern and penalizes it
  similarly to a direct link. Any pre-2026 source recommending this is
  stale —
  [writio.ai](https://writio.ai/blog/how-linkedin-algorithm-changed-2026-what-to-do).
- **Format engagement rates** (Richard van der Blom's Algorithm Insights +
  Sprout Social 2026 data): Document/PDF carousel 6.60% (highest), native
  video (30-90s) 5.60%, image+text 3.20%, text-only 2.00%, polls collapsed
  to 0.07% after the March 2026 "Authenticity Update" —
  [dataslayer.ai](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now).
- **Hashtags**: LinkedIn removed hashtag-following in 2024. Sources
  disagree sharply on remaining value — "roughly neutral" to "up to 30%
  engagement boost," a 15x spread — treat as low-priority regardless:
  [sproutsocial.com](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/linkedin-hashtags/),
  [contentin.io](https://contentin.io/blog/do-hashtags-work-on-linkedin/).
- **Signal hierarchy**: saves reportedly ~5x the reach of a like, comments
  ~2x — single-source claim, not independently replicated —
  [blog.hootsuite.com](https://blog.hootsuite.com/linkedin-algorithm/).
- **Native Newsletter feature**: reportedly bypasses feed-algorithm
  filtering, delivering directly via notification/email. Single-source
  claim — test cautiously, don't bet the strategy on it —
  [dataslayer.ai](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now).
- **Cadence**: weekly-or-tighter posting reportedly ~2x engagement vs.
  irregular schedule for personal profiles; timing advice conflicts across
  sources (different sources give different "optimal" windows) — don't
  over-index on any single timing claim.

## Growth tactics, ranked by evidence strength

1. **Substack Recommendations** — strongest evidence, platform-reported,
   mechanistically plausible.
2. **Restacking/replying to others' Notes** — mechanistically tied to the
   confirmed audience-overlap algorithm.
3. **Guest posts / cross-posting with adjacent newsletters** — logical,
   widely practiced, but evidence is anecdotal practitioner testimony, not
   measured studies. Plausibly high-value in a small, cross-linked expert
   niche like privacy/AI-governance.
4. **Podcast appearances** — same evidentiary status as guest posts.
5. **Clear "who am I, why should you care" framing for new subscribers** —
   a churn-reduction tactic, not acquisition, but matters because
   Notes/Recommendations-sourced subscribers arrive with less context than
   intentional ones and unsubscribe fast if disoriented.

## Common mistakes (for expert writers with good writing, small audience)

Publish-and-forget with no Notes engagement; assuming existing followers see
new posts by default (no longer guaranteed post-2025 algorithm change);
using the now-penalized LinkedIn link-in-comment trick; over-investing in
hashtags; treating Google/SEO as a primary acquisition channel given the
zero-click/AI Overview shift; no reintroduction framing for new/casual
readers; overcomplicating format by copying large newsletters'
multi-section structure; inconsistent cadence.
